Exploring Pedestrian Safety and Walkability in Downtown San Diego

Brian Sun, Colin Tran, Maxime Chung, Christian Kimayong | DSC 106, Spring 2026

Little Italy to Seaport Village is consistently the safest route, while Gaslamp to Convention Center shows the largest day/night shift. Crime density explains most variation across routes.

1. Start with distance

At first, the shortest route seems like the obvious choice.

2. Add crime data

Once crime exposure is added, some routes become less comfortable.

3. Add foot traffic

Busy routes may feel safer because there are more people nearby.

4. Switch to night

At night, lighting and activity levels become more important.

5. Final comparison

The safest route depends on what factors you care about most.
